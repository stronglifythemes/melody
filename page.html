About Melody

A boutique recording studio built around one idea: artists make their best music when they feel comfortable, heard and free to experiment.

Melody started as a small rehearsal room and grew into a full recording space for independent artists and labels. Today we record, produce, mix and master music across many genres, always with the same attention to detail.

Our approach

Every project begins with a conversation. We listen to your demos, understand the sound you imagine and plan the sessions around it. Technology supports the process, but the song always leads.

What we offer

  • Recording for vocals, instruments and full bands.
  • Production and arrangement from demo to release.
  • Mixing and mastering for singles, EPs and albums.

The room

Our live room and vocal booth are acoustically treated to sound natural and warm. A careful mix of analog and digital equipment gives each record depth and character.
